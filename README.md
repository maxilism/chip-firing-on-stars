# Labeled chip-firing on stars

Place labeled chips at the center of a star with ordered arms and fire until the chips stabilize. Which tableaux can occur, and how likely are they?

- [Reachability of middle orders in chip-firing on stars](papers/middle-orders.pdf) gives a decision procedure for the middle column with three chips per arm and a fixed number of arms. With extra arms, the permutations that occur as patterns are exactly the 321-avoiders.
- [Catalan growth of middle orders in chip-firing on stars](papers/density.pdf) proves that a fixed positive fraction of all 321-avoiders of each length occur as whole middle orders.
- [Inserting a batch in labeled chip-firing on stars](papers/column-insertion.pdf) proves that adding one batch forces rowwise insertion. With at least three arms and three chips per arm, at least 1/82 of distinct reachable tableaux are nonstandard.
- [The probability of a standard tableau in labeled chip-firing on stars](papers/random-firing.pdf) proves that with three chips per arm, the probability of a standard tableau tends to zero as the number of arms grows.
