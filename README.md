# Labeled chip-firing on stars

Place $3k$ labeled chips at the center of a star with $k$ ordered arms. After stabilization, read the middle chip on each arm in arm order. Which permutations occur, and how many are there?

- [Reachability of middle orders in chip-firing on stars](papers/middle-orders.pdf) gives a decision procedure for a fixed number of arms. With extra arms, the permutations that occur as patterns are exactly the 321-avoiders.
- [Catalan growth of middle orders in chip-firing on stars](papers/density.pdf) proves that a fixed positive fraction of all 321-avoiders of each length occur as whole middle orders.
